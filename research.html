Research

What reduces disease, in practice.

My work runs from pragmatic trials to simulation models — connected by a single question: which interventions actually lower risk when delivered through real health systems.

01

Digital lifestyle modification for type 2 diabetes

ICMR-DPCI · pragmatic cluster-randomised trial · 2025–present

I'm a core team member on the ICMR-DPCI study, a multicentre hybrid type 2 pragmatic cluster-randomised trial evaluating a WhatsApp-based lifestyle-modification programme delivered through urban primary health centres in Chennai and Jabalpur.

I lead the statistical analysis pipeline — cluster-robust ANCOVA, linear and generalised mixed models, multiple imputation for missing data, and difference-in-differences analyses of effect modification — and built the reproducible analysis infrastructure in R (targets + Quarto), including scoring algorithms, derived clinical variables, and publication-ready tables. I coordinate with multi-site investigators and implementation partners including Arogya World India Trust and the Greater Chennai Corporation.

  • Pragmatic trials
  • Mixed-effects models
  • Missing data
  • Reproducibility
  • Digital health
02

Point-of-care HbA1c diagnostics

Systematic review & meta-analysis · diagnostic test accuracy

I conduct evidence-synthesis work in diabetes diagnostics, including a systematic review and meta-analysis of point-of-care HbA1c test accuracy. The work covers risk-of-bias assessment, bivariate meta-analysis of diagnostic accuracy, and GRADE certainty assessment — aimed at informing how diabetes is detected in decentralised, real-world settings.

  • Meta-analysis
  • Diagnostic test accuracy
  • Risk of bias
  • GRADE
03

The diabetes care cascade

Population survey analysis

Using population survey data, I characterise the diabetes care cascade — quantifying attrition between diagnosis, treatment, and control to locate where people fall out of care and where interventions could have the most impact.

  • Care cascade
  • Survey analysis
  • NCD epidemiology
04

Tuberculosis transmission modelling

MPH thesis · agent-based model · Julia

For my MPH thesis, I designed an agent-based model simulating TB transmission in a dynamic synthetic population of Thiruvananthapuram (~100,000 agents) — specifying natural-history parameters, agent behaviours, and daily contact patterns, and collaborating with a programmer on the Julia implementation.

I integrated state TB registry data for baseline disease states and model calibration, then simulated TB incidence from 2025–2030 under varying diagnostic delays and coverage of household screening and preventive therapy — framing the work as simulation-based policy analysis.

  • Agent-based modelling
  • Transmission dynamics
  • Synthetic populations
  • Policy analysis
